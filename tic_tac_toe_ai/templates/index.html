How the AI Decides Its Moves
1. Easy: Plays randomly across available squares without checking winning lines.
2. Medium (Rule-Based): Evaluates predefined tactical heuristics:
• Priority 1: If AI has 2 in a row, take the 3rd to win immediately.
• Priority 2: If Player has 2 in a row, block the winning cell.
• Priority 3: Control center (cell 5), then corners, then edges.
3. Hard (Minimax): Recursively evaluates every possible future move branch. Scores terminal states (+10 for AI win, -10 for Player win, 0 for draw) discounted by tree depth. With optimal play, the AI is mathematically impossible to beat.
